The Voice-Powered Calculator

VOCULUS

SPEAK. CALCULATE. SIMPLIFY.

What if you could calculate without pressing a single button? VOCULUS transforms spoken mathematical expressions into answers using speech recognition, Python and physical servo-driven display segments.
