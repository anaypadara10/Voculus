01 / ABOUT VOCULUS

A new way to interact with mathematics.

Traditional calculators rely on buttons and keypads. VOCULUS explores another approach: speaking a mathematical expression and seeing the answer appear on a physical display.

The project brings together speech recognition, Python-based arithmetic processing, serial communication and an Arduino-controlled seven-segment-style display.

Our idea is simple: make calculation an experience that starts with speaking.
