OUR JOURNEY

From an idea to a working voice-powered calculator.

Every great project starts with an idea.

VOCULUS was developed through planning, experimenting, coding, assembling and testing. Here's a glimpse into our journey.

01 · The Idea

We imagined a calculator that could understand spoken mathematical expressions instead of relying on a traditional keypad.

02 · Planning & Design

We planned the seven-segment display, the electronic components and the software needed to bring our idea to life.

03 · Coding & Electronics

We worked with Python and Arduino Mega, connecting the software to the servo-powered display.

04 · Building the Model

We assembled the physical display and worked on arranging its segments and servo motors.

05 · Testing & Improvement

We tested the components, checked the display movements and improved the project step by step.

06 · Ready to Present

After bringing the software and hardware together, VOCULUS was ready to be presented at our science exhibition.

BUILT WITH CURIOSITY. POWERED BY INNOVATION.

Every test, challenge and improvement helped turn our idea into VOCULUS.
