LIVE DEMONSTRATION

One spoken expression. One calculated answer.

Experience the VOCULUS process

Here's an example of how a spoken mathematical expression becomes a numerical answer on the physical display.

STEP 1 · SPOKEN INPUT
“Twenty-five plus seventeen”
↓
STEP 2 · CALCULATION
25 + 17
↓
STEP 3 · FINAL ANSWER
42
DISPLAYED BY VOCULUS
🎙️ SpeakSay a mathematical expression into the microphone.
💻 ProcessPython recognises and processes the spoken expression.
🧮 CalculateThe program calculates the result.
🔢 DisplayArduino controls the servos to show the answer.

This is a visual demonstration of the process. To see the actual servo-powered display in action, visit our physical VOCULUS model at the exhibition!
